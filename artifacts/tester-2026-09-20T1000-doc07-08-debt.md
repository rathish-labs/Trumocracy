## 4. Decisions made, and why

1. **Suite placement — §5 `TS-ADV-01…16`, `ADV-02 · RISK-02`, following the TC-3564..TC-3576
   precedent.** The caveat is recorded in the Doc 07 changelog rather than left implicit: `RISK-02`
   is *coercion and vote-buying*, and in substance a false **enrolment** claim sits closer to
   `RISK-06` (deanonymisation) and `RISK-07` (state compulsion) — a citizen who believes "the
   document never leaves your phone" enrols under a threat model that does not hold. The FR-131/
   FR-132 honesty family is kept in one suite for traceability; **no case is offered as evidence
   about coercion**, and §5's RISK-02 verdict is untouched. **Doc 04 §14's `TS-V1-ID` suite was
   deliberately NOT opened** — it is reserved for the *built* verify-and-discard mechanics, and
   populating it with cases that verify the capability's **absence** would misrepresent the
   enrolment sprint's starting position.

2. **The requirement these cases verify is `FR-132 §(d)`, NOT `FR-131` clause (e) — and this is the
   single most load-bearing ruling of the session.** Doc 02 §4.45 clause (e) closes by placing
   **personhood-enrolment and identity-verification claims expressly outside** it (FR-132 §(d);
   §16.4 H-16/H-17/H-18; Doc 02 §13 routing (j)). The `/verify` page is the enrolment surface and
   all five retired claims are enrolment claims. **So not one of the fifteen carries an FR-131
   link**, the §3.1 FR-131 row is not edited, and the non-change is recorded once at Doc 08 §7
   entry 117 so a reader does not read silence as an oversight. TC-3585 and TC-3591 do apply
   FR-131's four-word list, but as an **instrument** over copy that clause (e) does not govern —
   both rows say so in terms.

3. **Every row states its own scope limits** (the TC-3573 / TC-3575 standard). The sharpest:
   - **TC-3577** asserts the flag **default in the registry**, not the state of any deployed
     environment; a runtime override would defeat it and no test here would see it (sre / Doc 10).
   - **TC-3583** — `SiteHeader` carries no `/verify` anchor, but **the home CTA still links there**
     (Doc 06 §7 item 28); the case does not claim otherwise and there is no repo-wide link sweep.
   - **TC-3584** records in terms that the five retired claims **still render with the flag on**;
     if the `dev`-only default ever flips, this case still passes and the product becomes
     dishonest. TC-3577 is the guard that matters.
   - **TC-3587** is the right fact on the **wrong surface for §(d)'s own wording**: §(d) puts the
     same-document-deduplication statement on "**the FR-131 honesty notice (DES-098)**", and
     DES-098 does not exist. The case proves the fact is stated honestly somewhere reachable, not
     that the §(d) notice duty is discharged.
   - **TC-3590** asserts "not identical and not empty", **not** that the Arabic says what the
     English says; and it guards the claim, not the fluency — `ARABIC-I18N` native-speaker review
     is still owed. **NFR-013 is NOT verified** (same ground as TC-3567/TC-3573).
   - **TC-3591** bans **Latin-script** words only; no Arabic-script claim is banned, and Doc 06
     v2.7.0 removed the bare `سري` ban on purpose.
   - **TC-3586** is an **enumerated** four-string jargon scan, not the scanner NFR-023 waits for;
     the NFR-023 row stays `G-UI` with Complete **0**.
