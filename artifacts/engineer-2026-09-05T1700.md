## Decisions made (and why)

1. **PrivacyStatus title is backing-aware, not blanket-neutral.** FR-131's ban is on describing
   *v1* voting; a ZK backing that declares `unlinkable: true` is the one case where "private" is
   true of the ballot, and clause 7 already selects the subtitle that way. The v2 row of the
   Doc 03 §10.12.3 sub-table is therefore still matched verbatim; only the v1 row deviates, by
   approver direction (2026-09-05) over the SDD v2.7.0 "status visibility" reading. Recorded as
   a deviation in Doc 06 §4a; SDD cascade owed to the architect.
2. **Negation-aware regression test rather than a blanket word ban.** FR-131(a) *mandates*
   "NOT anonymous, NOT receipt-free, NOT coercion-resistant", so a plain substring ban would fail
   the mandated text. UT-0887 bans the words unless immediately preceded by "not", bans
   "private"/"secure" outright, and separately asserts the (a)/(b)/(c) content — the retired
   body ("Nobody can see that a vote was yours") carries no banned word, so the positive
   assertions are what catch it. The two `it`s are the guard together; neither alone is.
3. **Scope held to FR-131 "v1 voting behaviour".** "Supporters are anonymous" (FR-082 tier
   property) and "your name kept private" (petition endorsement, en + ar) were left in place and
   routed, not silently rewritten — changing them is the product-owner's call, not a defect fix.
4. **Arabic fixed in the same drop.** The sweep found the Arabic banner carried the same lie; a
   fix that left one locale lying would not close REL-LIM-18.
