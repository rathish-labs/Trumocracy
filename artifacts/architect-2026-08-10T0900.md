### Contradictions recorded

**OI-13 (FR-062 vs NFR-001/NFR-024/TD-02):** Recorded in §18 as a DESIGN-LEVEL CONTRADICTION.
Design-side consequence stated honestly: the `identityCommitment` is the only durable
per-person identifier; a participation profile that reveals party membership makes it
the linking mechanism for the exact linkage NFR-001 prohibits. There is no privacy-preserving
mechanism that simultaneously satisfies both FR-062 and NFR-001 as written. The resolution is
for the human approver (Rathish) at Gate 1 re-affirmation. DES-064 is designed but flagged
off above dev until OI-13 is resolved.

**OI-12 (FR-073 vs ADR-003):** Resolved by ADR-016 explicitly amending ADR-003 for Phase 1.
No unresolved contradiction — ADR-016 is a Phase-1 restriction; ADR-003's 1-of-N model is
explicitly stated to resume at Phase 3+ per a future ADR with threat model and audit.
Recorded as resolved in §16 open questions.
