## 1. The ruling, in one paragraph

**Both `anon` strings FAIL FR-131 clause (e) and require v1 variants.** The v2.13.0 `anon` TITLE
disposition ruled "Anonymous" COMPLIANT on one load-bearing premise — *"The distinction is the
voter, not the word"*: the badge renders only for open-tier users who cannot cast a binding vote
under FR-122/FR-123, so it cannot be describing that user's **voting** behaviour. **That premise
is true and remains true**, and it is exactly why the disposition does not survive: it was an
answer to a **voting-scoped** ban, and clause (e) (Doc 02 v2.17.0, unchanged at **v2.17.3**)
extends the duty to "any other v1 participation act", naming **joining or belonging to a party**
and **endorsing or backing a petition** — two of the three contexts Doc 03 clause 8 itself assigns
the pill (screens 1.6 and 2.3). FR-122/FR-123 are unamended; **what changed is the requirement,
not the voter.** The subtitle "Nothing you do here is linked to you" fails more plainly: it is a
universal negative that an ordinary grade-8 reader on the join screen takes to mean Trumocracy
cannot link them to the act — the exact conclusion clause (e) forbids — and §10.12.3 has conceded
since v2.7.1 that it is not literally true in v1. Its v2.7.1 rescue read "linked" as "***publicly***
linked" on the strength of FR-124(b)'s **publication** policy; clause (e) is not a publication
rule and expressly makes the reader's reading govern over the author's gloss. The safe harbour
saves neither string: it is expressly "**in the same string**", and clause 8's affordance is both a
different artefact and **unbuilt**.

## 2. The finding that turned out bigger than the debt

**No single static `anon` subtitle can be honest across clause 8's three contexts.** On browsing
(1.2) and joining (1.6), "not made public" is true (FR-124(b)). On **endorsing (2.3) it is false**,
because petition endorsement is **public by design** — Doc 14 §2.2, "a public act, on purpose";
"only back a petition if you are comfortable being seen to support it"; `private_endorsement` is a
Phase-4 charter flag OFF in every v1 deployment. **Any single-string fix trades a clause-(e) breach
in one direction for one in the other**, and the direction it would break is the one act in the set
where exposure is the whole point. `OPEN-27` reads like a two-string word swap; it is not. The
`anon` copy must be **context-selected**, by the same fail-honest discipline clauses 7 and 9 apply
to `ver`. On screen 2.3 the title has a **second, independent** failure that does not depend on the
reader test at all: clause (e) forbids describing a public-by-design act as kept private, secret or
**hidden**, and "Anonymous" describes it as hidden.
