## What was done

### Task 1 — Doc 03 §10.12.3 DES-094 backing-aware copy (v2.6.1 → v2.7.0)

**Document:** `docs/03-architecture-design-sdd.md` — bumped to v2.7.0 (Status: In Review, Last updated: 2026-08-25).

**(a) v1 `ver`-state copy decided:**

- **Title: "Verified — private"** — RETAINED unchanged.
  - FR-131 banned-words analysis: the word "private" in this title describes the *visibility of the holder's verified status* (private to the holder; never published per-individual; aggregate-only enforced by FR-124(b)), NOT voting behaviour. FR-131's ban applies only to descriptions of v1 voting behaviour (§10.13.6 DES-098). COMPLIANT; no title change required.
- **v1 subtitle: "Your vote counts. How you voted is never made public."**
  - Truthful for v1: aggregate-only publication is policy-enforced by FR-124(b); individual vote direction is never published to any public audience. Does not claim identity is unstored. Passes all four FR-131 banned-words checks (no "private", "anonymous", "receipt-free", "secure"). Grade-8 reading level. One short sentence.
  - DES-098/FR-131 separately discloses the operator-level DB access — the pill copy and the honesty notice are complementary, not substitutes.
- **v2 subtitle: "Your vote counts. Your identity is not stored."** — RETAINED for v2 ZK backing (unlinkable = true). False for v1; accurately describes the ZK backing (no identity at rest, no account↔membership DB link survives).

**(b) All three states analysed for backing-sensitivity:**

- **`ver` state:** backing-sensitive — v1 and v2 subtitles differ (see above). Backing-aware sub-table and clause 7 added.
- **`anon` state ("Nothing you do here is linked to you"):** honesty tension analysed. In v1, `anon`-state users have a phone_hash in the DB. However, "linked to you" is reasonably interpreted as "linked to your publicly identifiable real-world self" — which phone_hash alone does not establish. FR-124(b) aggregate-only policy means no individual action is ever published linked to any identity. DES-098 covers the platform's broader data practices. **Verdict: no v1 variant needed.** Analysis recorded in §10.12.3.
- **`pub` state ("You chose a public role. Your record is visible"):** backing-independent. True in both v1 and v2. Voluntary role-taking (FR-124(c)). No banned words. No identity-at-rest claims. **Verdict: no v1 variant needed.**

**(c) Clause 7 (normative, added to DES-094 binding list):**

Subtitle selection for `ver` MUST be keyed off `IEligibilityVerifier.getProperties().unlinkable` (DES-095 seam, §10.13.2). v2 subtitle renders only when `unlinkable = true`. All other cases — including `unlinkable = false`, call failure, or absent backing — fall back to the v1 (weaker-claim) subtitle. **Fail-honest default is the v1 copy, never the v2 copy.** Cites FR-131, H-15, H-16, T-01, T-02.

**(d) Other changes:**
- DES-094 element-table traces updated to add FR-131.
