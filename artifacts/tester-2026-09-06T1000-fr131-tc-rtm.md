## 3. The FR-131 closure ruling — asked plainly, answered plainly

**Does the FR-131 Must row close? Does 16 → 17? NO.** FR-131 (SRS v2.16.3 §4.45) imposes eleven
distinct obligations. I ruled each against the evidence that now exists:

| # | Obligation | Ruling | Evidence / reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notice displayed **wherever a vote is cast**, before the ballot is confirmed | **NOT MET** | SCR-13 (ballot booth) and SCR-14 (post-vote confirmation) are unbuilt (Doc 06 §7 #21). A notice cannot be shown wherever a vote is cast in a product where no vote can be cast |
| 2 | Content (a): conventional auth; NOT anonymous / receipt-free / coercion-resistant | **MET — copy layer** | UT-0887 → TC-3565, en and ar |
| 3 | Content (b): the platform DB CAN see vote direction and party membership | **MET — copy layer** | TC-3565; also TC-3535/UT-0869 (join copy) |
| 4 | Content (c): the private ballot arrives with the v2 layer, not on yet | **MET — copy layer** | TC-3565 ("not switched on yet") |
| 5 | Clause (d): open-tier non-counting disclosure, 4 sub-clauses, non-dismissable, before refusal | **MET AT TWO SURFACES, NOT AT THE BALLOT** | Parties-directory (TC-3534/UT-0864) and proposals ballot-admission (UT-0881/UT-0882). Clause (d) names "casting a binding vote"; that surface does not exist. **TC-3481 stays Blocked** |
| 6 | Visible **before confirmation** | **NOT VERIFIABLE** | There is no confirmation step for it to precede |
| 7 | Non-dismissable — **"the voter MUST acknowledge the notice to proceed"** | **NOT MET — decisive** | The banner is non-dismissable (no dismiss control, asserted) but has **no acknowledge control at all**: nothing to acknowledge, nothing gating "proceed". Doc 06 §7 item 26(d) records it as owed SCR-13 story scope |
| 8 | WCAG 2.2 AA (DES-081) + screen-reader accessible | **NOT EVIDENCED** | No a11y gate, no screen-reader pass; NFR-011 is G-UI. `role="note"` and UT-0704's accessible name are not AA conformance |
| 9 | Appears on **SCR-13 and SCR-14** | **NOT MET** | Both unbuilt |
| 10 | Closing sentence: the four banned words; no v2-guarantee claims | **NOW MET for the swept code, and GUARDED** | TC-3564..TC-3567 (rendered banner + en/ar source), TC-3568 (`ver` title), TC-3569 (flag description), over the engineer's sweep of `packages/*/src` and `apps/web/src`. **Not** met as stated for "README and all public-facing materials" — no test covers those |
| 11 | Seam half the RTM row also tracks (cast / silent change / tally-hash / refusal / embargo / audit publication) | **5 of 6 at `IS_INSECURE_MOCK=true`** | TC-3482..TC-3486 pass; **TC-3487 Blocked** (audit-contract endpoint), TC-3476 Blocked |

**Verdict: FR-131 stays OPEN — `G-PHASE3`.** Six of eleven obligations are unmet or unevidenced,
and **two are unmet by absence of the thing itself, not absence of a test**: the acknowledge-to-
proceed control does not exist, and the ballot surfaces do not exist. **Obligation 7 alone would
keep the row open even if every surface were built.** What this drop genuinely closes is FR-131's
*closing sentence* — and it closes it well, with regression guards on the rendered copy, the source
strings, both locales, the status badge and the flag ledger. Recording that as a **row closure**
would be the single most misleading thing the matrix could do about FR-131, because the clause that
most protects a voter — you must acknowledge before you proceed — is precisely the one still
missing. A row that closes on the easy clause of a requirement teaches every downstream reader that
the hard clauses are optional.
