## Fourth rework — Doc 03 v2.4.1 → v2.5.0 (2026-08-23, ID-verification ruling)

**Source ruling:** DECISIONS-2026-08-23-V1-IDENTITY-VERIFICATION.md (Rathish, 2026-08-23).
**Doc 02 base pinned:** v2.10.0 (133 FR / 131 active / 114 Must; 21 BR; 28 NFR; 15 CON). PO increment to v2.11.0 OWED — architect answers gate PO's next version.

### Q-1 answer (recorded)

ID-verification providers return: `status`, `verification_id` (ephemeral), `subject_id` (stable pseudonymous token), `checks.age_verified`, `checks.issuing_country`, `extracted_data` (PII fields). Platform STORES: `id_verified_flag`, `age_verified`, `issuing_region`, `subject_id_hash` (HMAC-SHA-256/KMS-pepper), `phone_hash` (HMAC-SHA-256/KMS-pepper), `verified_at` — restricted-class credential store only. Platform DISCARDS: document images, biometric templates, name, DOB, document number, expiry date, selfie frames, ephemeral `verification_id`. `subject_id_hash` enables same-document deduplication (prevents same-document-different-phone reuse). Tension: verify-and-discard vs uniqueness — `subject_id_hash` is a derived identifier (deepens T-07 slightly); legal classification routed to CON-015. Doc 01 §E1 delivered by this allowlist. Recommendation: retain `subject_id_hash` for best Sybil resistance within verify-and-discard constraint.

### Q-2 answer (recorded)

`phone_hash = HMAC-SHA-256(E.164-normalized(phone), pepper_phone)`; `subject_id_hash = HMAC-SHA-256(subject_id, pepper_id)`. Two peppers in KMS/HSM. Slow KDFs (bcrypt/Argon2id) rejected — non-deterministic salts make deduplication lookup impossible at scale. Residual: Indian mobile number space ~4 × 10⁹; exhaustible in hours with GPU if both DB dump AND pepper are compromised. Accepted residual. Operational MUST requirements: KMS-only (no raw key in app memory); dual-authorization; rotation schedule; log scrubbing; application-layer rate limiting on hash-lookup endpoints.

### Q-3 answer (recorded)

Architect-decidable: denylist, allowlist, HMAC/KMS, restricted-class restriction, fail-closed mode. CON-015 / GDPR counsel required: (1) DPDP ephemeral provider processing compliance; (2) Aadhaar Act — is non-UIDAI document check authorized?; (3) phone_hash / subject_id_hash as personal data under DPDP/GDPR; (4) retention period; (5) cross-border transfer; (6) erasure rights vs audit chain; (7) EU GDPR Article 9 for political platform (Phase 2+). CON-015 now CRITICAL PATH — no enrolment sprint begins without it cleared for government-ID check path.

### FR-004 plurality question (architect-resolved)

v1 ID-verification provider is application-layer, NOT a protocol-level attestor in the FR-004/ADR-003/ADR-016/ADR-021 sense. FR-004's ≥2 attestors requirement satisfied at protocol level by ADR-021/OI-20. The concentration risk is real and recorded as T-08 (Phase-1 dated limitation, not permanent; FR-129 prevents entrenchment). Not a blocking approver decision.
