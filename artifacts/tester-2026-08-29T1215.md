## Doc 07 → v2.3.2, and the TC-mint decision

**I minted TC-3541** (next free id after TC-3540). This was a judgement call, and I did not make it
to look thorough — I made it because of a specific traceability hole:

1. **`PREREQ-01`'s exit criterion *is* a test.** DES-101 §10.13.10.1 rule 6 names the adversarial
   amendment test as the closing evidence, and the approver made passing it the condition for
   shipping the entire on-chain governance increment. If that test has no TC, then the exit
   criterion for a blocking prerequisite lives only in a design document — untracked in the suite
   that is supposed to prove it. That is precisely the artifact-bus failure the handbook warns
   about.
2. **FR-077's RTM row had no TC covering the clause that keeps it open.** TC-3403 is titled around
   publication refusal; TC-3508..TC-3510 are explicitly the publication half. Until now the row's
   stated reason pointed at nothing in Doc 07.
3. Doc 07 already carries 175 Blocked and 49 No-mechanism cases by design — recording a case the
   product cannot yet satisfy is this document working correctly, not padding.

**Status: `No mechanism`, not `Blocked`** — and the distinction matters. Blocked means the
instrument doesn't exist. Here the instrument (the EVM harness) exists and the case is
**executable today; it would FAIL**. Per §0.1 that makes it a defect against the **requirement**,
not against the case, which is exactly what FR-077's open row asserts. DES-101 rule 6 says the same
thing: "written against today's code that test fails, which is the point."

The case covers both failure modes: an amendment naming an **unrelated** clause installing a charter
with the non-violence clause stripped (the structural one), and the direct variant on a party whose
founders never entrenched the clause.
