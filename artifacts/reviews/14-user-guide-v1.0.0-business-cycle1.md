## 1. Summary (BLUF)

Doc 14 v1.0.0 (`UG-TRUMOCRACY`, In Review, last updated **2026-08-09**) is a genuinely
well-crafted piece of plain-language writing — Diátaxis structure, grade-8 register, no
cryptographic jargon, a strong "what this is not" section, and a §3.2 numeric reference whose
petition, tier, surge, fork, anonymity-set and sponsorship values I checked line by line against
`packages/protocol/src/constants.js` and found **accurate**. It nonetheless **FAILS** this review
at **54%** with **six Critical issues**, and the single reason is the one a user guide exists to
avoid: **it describes a product that does not exist, and it does so in exactly the places where a
citizen's physical safety is at stake.**

The decisive finding is **FR-131**. SRS v2.16.3 §4.45 states: *"The v1 product — its UI, README,
and all public-facing materials — MUST NOT use the words 'private', 'anonymous', 'receipt-free',
or 'secure' to describe v1 voting behaviour"*, and requires a plain-language notice stating v1
voting is **NOT anonymous**, **NOT receipt-free**, **NOT coercion-resistant**, and that **the
platform database CAN see vote direction and party membership**. A user guide is public-facing
material. This guide instead builds its central §2.6 warning on the claim that *"Your vote in this
version is **anonymous**"* and answers *"True in version 0.1.0? **Yes.**"* — the forbidden word,
asserting the opposite of the mandated disclosure. It then repeats the inversion in §0.2, §2.3,
§3.5, §3.6 and §4.3, including the claim that **no party member list exists** — a claim the
engineer has already **retracted from the shipped product** as a v1 honesty defect
(`apps/web/src/i18n/en.ts`, `joinPrivate`, with the in-code comment that the earlier
*"nobody gets that list, including us"* copy *"was true only of the v2 backing and is NOT true of
the v1 conventional backing"*), and whose removal the RTM records as the FR-131 clause (b) fix
(Doc 08 §3.1 FR-131 row, UT-0869 / TC-3535). **Doc 14 is now the last artefact in the repository
still carrying the retracted claim.**

Alongside that, the guide's §2 / §3.1 / §3.4 availability tables mark as *"Available in 0.1.0?
Yes"* four capabilities the RTM (v2.7.0 Approved — **122 of 138 Must rows OPEN**) and Doc 06
§7 record as unbuilt: **voting/result screens** (SCR-13/SCR-14 owed, Doc 06 §7 #21; TC-3481
**Blocked**), **account recovery** (Doc 06 §7 #11 *"No recovery path is implemented"*),
**residency confirmation** (FR-008 `G-NOMECH` — *"no residency-change function and no cooldown
exist"*), and **manifesto promises with version history** (FR-094/FR-095 — *"no implementation"*).
The guide also describes the **v2 ZK personhood enrolment** as the shipped Task 1 while omitting
the entire **v1 model that is actually built** (FR-132 phone SMS + government-ID check;
FR-122/FR-123 open tier vs counting tier; the FR-131 clause (d) notice that ships today at the
parties directory) — so a reader is told their vote counts when in v1 it may not.

Nothing here impugns the writing. The fix is not stylistic: the guide must be re-scoped from
"the finished product, minus privacy" to **what release 0.1.0 actually is**.
