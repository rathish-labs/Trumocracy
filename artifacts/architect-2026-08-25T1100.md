### ISS-01 (Medium) — `anon`-state copy analysis rework

**Resolution: option (a) — normative disclosure obligation (clause 8 added).**

The v2.7.0 `anon` analysis was replaced with a multi-paragraph analysis in §10.12.3:

1. User class stated explicitly: authenticated open-tier (phone-verified, NOT government-ID-verified)
   users. This is the user class for whom the claim is most consequential.

2. Interpretive basis stated explicitly: "Nothing you do here is linked to you" is sustained only
   in the sense of "publicly linked to your real-world self through any published record" — NOT
   "unreadable by the operator." In v1, all open-tier actions (party joins, endorsements, indexer
   browsing events) ARE associated with the DB account. The `phone_hash` (HMAC-SHA-256/KMS-pepper)
   is held in restricted-class storage. The operator with the KMS pepper CAN derive the phone number.
   In the India pilot, the TRAI SIM-registration mandate (ADR-025 "Why phone over email") makes the
   chain `account → phone_hash → phone number → TRAI-registered real identity` a concrete,
   subpoena-accessible path (Doc 02 H-16, H-18; §10.13.7 T-01, T-02). This is not theoretical.

3. Disclosure gap acknowledged: DES-098 (FR-131 honesty notice) applies at vote time (SCR-13/14)
   only. The `anon` pill renders on browsing (screen 1.2), joining (screen 1.6), endorsing (screen 2.3)
   with no equivalent contextual disclosure at the point of the claim.

4. Resolution — option (a) adopted: clause 8 added to DES-094 normative binding list. Rationale:
   design debt (option b) would defer the obligation past the enrolment sprint; a normative clause
   enforces it at component-spec level. DES-098's scope (SCR-13/14) is unchanged.

5. `anon` subtitle — no v1 variant required. The subtitle is truthful under the stated interpretive
   basis (no `anon` action is published linked to any individual identity; FR-124(b) aggregate-only
   policy). Operator-side linkage is real in v1 but is a data-practices disclosure concern addressed
   by clause 8, not by a subtitle change. Decision recorded with a trigger: if future honesty review
   or user-research in the India pilot finds "publicly linked" is not the reading a reasonable user
   applies, a subtitle variant MUST be considered before that deployment.

**TRAI citation location:** ADR-025 "Why phone over email" section (not §(c-iv), which covers
SIM-swap attacks; §(c-ii) covers phone numbers at rest as identity data). Both cited correctly
in the reworked analysis.

### ISS-02 (Low) — clause 7 trigger annotation

Appended annotation to clause 7: `getProperties().unlinkable` is a proxy for the full "no identity
at rest" guarantee. The current v2 ZK backing that declares `unlinkable: true` also guarantees
"no identity data at rest" by construction (ZK enrolment; nullifier-only on-chain; no `phone_hash`
or `subject_id_hash` retained). Any future backing declaring `unlinkable: true` MUST satisfy
the same guarantee by design review before the v2 copy may render behind it. This is a
design-review invariant for future backing registrations.
