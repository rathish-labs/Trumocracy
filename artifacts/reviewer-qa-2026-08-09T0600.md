## Verified clean (checked, not assumed)

`transferOwnership` anywhere; the mechanical scan for `delegatecall`, `selfdestruct`, `assembly`,
`payable`, `receive()` and `fallback()` across `packages/contracts/src/` returns nothing; no
transferable membership and no weight field; `authoriseSpender` is correctly restricted;
nullifier scoping is sound at every call site except `execute`; the three root-history ring

`_ceilMulDiv` matches `Math.ceil`; `_sort` and `issuerSetValid()` are bounded and not
attacker-influenced. Where the absences fail, they fail through *authorised* callers (H-01,
C-06, M-04), which is precisely the residual Doc 06 §4 honestly says its ABI scanning cannot cover.
