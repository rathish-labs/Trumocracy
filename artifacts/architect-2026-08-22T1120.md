   privacy model — leak-check PASS; (b) SCR stubs — partially covered, gaps are design debt
   not conflicts; (c) ADR-011 packages/ui designation — sound, ADR-023 formalises adoption.

2. **ADR minted (ADR-023):** The adoption of a specific token set and privacy-status component
   for packages/ui is an architecture decision with normative consequences (FR-124 enforcement
   at component level, territory rule as design invariant). A Doc 03 section alone was not
   sufficient — the decision record establishes the "why" and alternatives rejected.

   to "OI-19". OI-19 is the invite-gating ruling (FR-125; DECISIONS-2026-08-20-OI19-OI20.md).
   The correct referent is the **FR-124 ruling** (Doc 02 v2.3.1, Rathish, 2026-08-20). All
   citations in §10.12 use FR-124. The mislabel is noted in §10.12.1 preamble and in ADR-023.
   No requirement was changed; the referent correction is purely citation accuracy.

4. **Leak-check result:** PASS. All 15 wireframe screens reviewed. All privacy-pill instances
   are self-view. All other-actor data is either aggregate-only (FR-124(b)-compliant) or
   Worker/Candidate-tier public data (FR-124(c)-permitted). Normative invariant added to
   DES-094: the `ver` state MUST NOT render on unauthenticated routes or other-actor surfaces.
