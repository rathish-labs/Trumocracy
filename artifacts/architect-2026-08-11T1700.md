credentials untouched by revocation unless separately voted.

### Contradictions noticed while writing ADRs

No real new contradictions. One observation recorded (not a contradiction):

The emergency revocation timelock (7 days) in ADR-020 uses the same duration as the
RECOVERY_DELAY constant in ADR-018. This is a deliberate consistency choice (the "minimum
non-zero emergency window" principle), not a coincidence or constraint. If RECOVERY_DELAY
were ever changed via the amendment boundary, the emergency revocation timelock would need
a separate change — these two constants are currently equal in value but independent in
governance. This observation is recorded here for the next architect session; it is NOT
recorded in ss18 because it is not a contradiction in the current design.
